My father taught me chess when I was eight or nine. I left it early and never went back.

At IIT Madras a close friend brought Die Siedler von Catan to the dorm, and we queued to play the four-player game. In graduate school I built a position around the 6s and 8s and watched seven 5s come up in a row. I stopped playing Catan.

Terra Mystica has no dice and no card luck, and each faction plays by its own rules. I learned it from faction guides and recorded tournament games.

Profile on Board Game Arena

Terra Mystica

In November 2020 I played the round of 24 of the Fire Two Open Season 2, a live Terra Mystica tournament streamed on Twitch. My group match, against ChristopherHo, jesseg and firexed, is on YouTube with commentary. In Gaia Project, its successor, I usually play the Geodens.

At the table

My advisor, Prof. Gautam Dasarathy, played Terra Mystica with the lab and enjoyed it. Aniket, a PhD colleague, learned Great Western Trail from me; in that game I once held a hand of four value-1 Jersey cows. In one Teotihuacan game I scored 163 points. My wife’s games are Splendor and Viticulture. After a long day we play Ghost Stories at home, which is cooperative.
