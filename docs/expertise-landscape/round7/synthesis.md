# Round 7 synthesis (Fable): keeping each field whole

Voices: Fable (with two prototypes), Sol (xhigh), Terra (high), Opus. All four agree on the rule, the drawing, the layout and the repository question; they differ only in constants.

## The rule, unanimous
Field terraces stay cumulative and are never altered by sharing. Sharing becomes exact-layer: a shared entry joins the fields it is tagged with at the layer where it is recorded, and nowhere else. Opus's sentence: "Each field keeps its whole stack. Two fields join at a layer only as far as they share entries at that layer." Width at that layer = the smaller field's terrace there times the shared fraction of that field's entries at that layer. Round 4's "arches are impossible" is overturned: a joint paper with no shared reading is an arch, connected at the summit and open beneath; sharing recorded at several layers stacks into a wall. Three-field entries draw one junction (or spanning-tree edges), never three pairwise bridges.

## The span
A slab, not a full terrace step: 0.35 of a step when it stands alone, a full step only when the same pair also shares the layer below, so walls read as walls (Opus). Deck in the layer's top tone with no seam where it lands; riser only on the viewer-facing edge in the layer's riser tones; no underside, no dimming of what lies beneath (all four: dimming is a cast shadow by another name and hides exactly the benches the owner wants kept). Drawn ring edge to ring edge, overshooting a few units into each terrace. Paint order interleaves spans into the per-level loop, lower levels first, so a higher summit in front still hides a low span behind it (Opus's correction to the proposal). Same-level spans never cross and no span passes over a third field; both are layout constraints and build assertions. A span is drawn only when its half-width is at least about 3 px; thinner shares are listed in the index as "1 shared cited entry with Bandits" rather than inflated into land. Queue-layer sharing draws nothing; the shelves already meet in the water.

## Layout
Collision on the cited terrace, the land shore, never on the reading shelf, which is what pushed Optimization away from Bandits. Gap 10 units between fields that share anything, 36 between fields that share nothing. Every sharing pair aims to sit shore to shore; attraction strength weights cited and read sharing above written and published, so fifteen shared readings pull harder than one joint paper. Never set distance from the highest shared layer (that is what swallowed Robotics). Use the directional shore radius, since the outline wobble reaches 1.4 times the nominal radius. Hard projection to the minimum distances after every relaxation sweep, third-field clearance for every span corridor, several seeded restarts, and a loud build failure if a sharing pair cannot be made adjacent. Positions are then locked.

## Honesty
Method page: "Each field is drawn with its whole stack. Where two fields share entries at a layer, a span joins them at that layer and nowhere else, as wide as the shared fraction of the smaller field's entries there. A joint paper with no shared reading is therefore an arch: connected at the summit, open beneath. A shared item is counted once in each field it belongs to; the span marks it as one item, not two." Caveats to print: thin shares below the drawing threshold are listed, not drawn; no connection means no shared entry in the ledger, not independence (Terra); span length carries no meaning.

## Prototype evidence
`proto7/build7.py` implements exact-layer spans and the land-shore layout on the placeholder data. Robotics, Hyperspectral and Catan keep complete skirts; the Bandits–Robotics paper is a thin slab from summit to summit over both fields' benches; Optimization–Hyperspectral shows a low cited saddle with a high span above it. The slab at a full step looked like a plank; at 0.35 of a step it reads as a bridge. A second prototype that leaned upper terraces toward the shared neighbour shortened spans by about fifteen of ninety units and was dropped. The plain stress solver left one overlap (Catan on Bandits' skirt), which the hard projection step fixes.

## Repository
Unanimous: not a separate repository now. A self-contained `portfolio/landscape/` folder with its own uv project, a JSON schema in and out, pure rule modules (widths and radii, shares and spans, layout, terrain, render), and pytest golden fixtures: a toy ledger of three fields with known shared entries and the expected radii, spans and distances. Estimates: Opus 1.5 to 2 days, Sol and Terra 2 to 4, Fable 1 to 2. The React component only paints the scene JSON. A separate repo costs another 4 to 10 days and pays back only when a second site uses it. Tests all four named: radii from known counts; a shared cited entry makes no higher span; a joint paper makes no lower span; 100 percent sharing fuses only that layer; a three-field entry makes one junction; the thin-share threshold; cited-shore gaps 10 and 36 with no shelf force; no span over a third field; deterministic output; a rendered snapshot.

## Do not
Never let sharing change a field's own radii or footprint (Terra, Fable). Never let one joint paper force two fields to be neighbours (Sol). Never fill the window in a wall (Opus): if Bandits and Robotics share cited, read and published but not written, the gap at written stays.
