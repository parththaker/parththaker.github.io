# Round 8 synthesis (Fable): the connector, and a stack panel instead of marks

Voices: Sol (xhigh), Terra (high), Opus (with the prototype renders), a research agent (USGS Bulletin 1393, NPS pages, Imhof and Tanaka summaries), and Fable with seven prototype variants in `proto8/`. Parth's verdict on the round-7 rectangle: "it looks like a plank connecting the two rather than something elegant". He preferred the organic round-4 saddles, which were rejected only for filling everything beneath the shared layer.

## A. Connector: unanimous on method, agreed on the arch, split only on tone

**Per-layer masks.** All three voices rank this first and the renders confirm it. For each layer k the mask is the union of (terraces at or above k) and (joins at exactly k), blurred once as the terraces are, then contoured at 0.5. The join thereby inherits the terrace contour language: filleted abutments from the blur, the same edge strokes, the same riser tones. When every layer of a pair is shared the stacked joins are a continuous terraced ridge, which is the round-4 saddle; when only the summit is shared there is a single deck at summit height with the lower terraces untouched. Nothing about a join may be drawn from any geometry other than the contoured mask (Opus's and Terra's "one thing not to do").

**Plan shape.** Waist 0.80 of the semantic half-width at mid-span (Sol 0.78, Opus 0.75–0.80, Terra 0.62; the prototype at 0.72 already read as a bone, so the gentler figure), eased with a smoothstep over the outer 35% of the length. Opus diagnosed the bone: summit knobs 2.5× the shaft, short fillets, and a dead-straight shaft whose parallel edges were the only straight lines on the map. Remedies adopted: a flare of 1.30 inside the outer 18% of the length, an edge wobble of ±8% of the half-width over two cycles with a different phase per side, and a bow of 9% of the length away from the plateau centre (Fable's arch3 prototype; the curved axis made the largest single difference). Rings under 40 px² after contouring are dropped (Opus spotted a stray blob).

**Length.** Sol and Terra proposed that a join connect the nearest points of the two layer-k contours and stop there rather than run to the mesa centres; that is already the construction (ring edge to ring edge, overlapping 6 units into each), so a summit join stays long. The length carries no meaning and the method page says so.

**The arch.** Where a join has nothing one step beneath it (neither ground nor a lower join), its viewer-facing riser opens: 0.80 of a step deep at the abutments, a half-ellipse over the central 70% of the clear span to 0.25 of a step at the crown, with a 3 px floor so it survives the overview (Opus). Where the layer below is also joined the riser is a full step and the stack reads as a wall. The soffit edge is stroked at 0.8 px, 60% of the shade tone (Terra). No cast shadow: all three voices and the research reject it as round 7's dimming in miniature, and the research found no cartographic precedent for arches in plan-oblique relief at all.

**Deck tone.** Sol: keep the layer tone, at most 4% darker at the waist; Terra: 92% lightness; Opus: 4% darker clipped to the join. Adopted: a 4.5% neutral shade over the join's own outline, fading to nothing at both abutments, drawn as a gradient so it leaves no seam.

## B. Stack panel: unanimous

Inline right column, not a Sheet (all three): at 1024 px and above the map takes 60% and the panel 40%; below that the panel sits under the map in page flow. One stack, not tabs, in terrace order top first: published, written, read, cited, to read. Each header is a 32 px band in its layer's tone with the count: `published · 5`. A band with a count of zero is shown disabled with no body, so no empty-state sentence is needed (Opus). Published and written open by default. The selected band has a 3 px rule in the site's red on its left; its terrace on the map gets a 1.6 px red outline following the field's own terrace edge (the join excluded), 40% on hover; deck tones and Tanaka strokes are never recoloured.

Published and written entries: the title as the link, then a mono ledger row `2022 · paper · NeurIPS 2022`, then `shared with Robotics` when tagged with a second field. Read, cited and to read default to counts and `12 shared with Optimization` lines; titles appear only when `list_titles:` in reading.yaml names the layer, and then they are public and crawlable for good (Sol's warning: collapsing in the browser is not privacy). Clicking a join selects the layer on the focused field and heads the band with `3 read entries shared with Robotics`, shared works first. Deep links: `#bandits`, `#bandits/published`, `#bandits/published/<slug>`, `#bandits--robotics/read`. The index table stays without its numeral column; its field cells link into the map. Nothing to add: no search or filters inside the panel (Sol), no hover cards (Terra), no numerals anywhere (Opus).
